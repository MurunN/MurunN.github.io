# SteppeQuest vNext 4 — Live Gameplay Upgrade

## Morin Urtuu
- 4 fully playable stages: Dawn Steppe, River Valley, Forest Pass, Night Station.
- Real-time curved road rendered procedurally; no cinematic background image is used.
- Road now has perspective strips, wheel/hoof ruts, small stones, roadside grass, fences, flags, gers, pine trees and stage-specific scenery.
- River stage includes moving river/ford visuals and mud/puddle slowdowns.
- Animated horse and courier: articulated 4-leg gallop, head/neck, mane, tail, rider lean and sprint effects.
- Working lane changes, jump, sprint, stamina, collisions, collectibles, stage goals, station gates, score, best score and pause/resume.
- Desktop + touch controls.

## Mounted Archery
- Real-time curved course rendered procedurally; no static game screenshot background.
- Detailed moving horse + mounted archer with articulated gallop and bow arm following the player's aim.
- Working lane movement, sprint/slow speed control, horse stamina, focus, bow draw power, wind, target approach speed, target types, combos, accuracy and best score.
- Three visually different rounds: open steppe, river crosswind, dusk master round.
- Dynamic road ruts, stones, flags, gers, river scenery, particles, dust, arrows and hit feedback.
- Pause/resume and desktop + touch controls.
